## Decision

Shortlist locations for further investigation rather than committing to a lease. Validate real demand, point-of-interest completeness, travel times, rents and unit economics first. The three weight settings are illustrative assumptions, not stakeholder-elicited preferences or confidence intervals.

## Limits

All locations and demand values are fictional. The rectangular extent is a screening box, not the Bogotá municipal boundary. Distances are straight-line great-circle distances, not walking or driving distances. Fewer nearby competitors is an explicit model assumption; clustering may also signal stronger demand. No external truth dataset was used to verify the synthetic coordinates.
